Where we work

A purpose-built facility in Burbank

In the heart of the post-production district, built around the careful work of saving fragile film, video, audio, and photographs. The facility's layout, equipment, and controlled working environment were designed around protecting irreplaceable originals. Located in the heart of Southern California's film community, our Burbank facility serves studios, archives, museums, universities, and private collections throughout Los Angeles and around the world.
