The process

Seeing the light

A faithfully captured image begins with the light itself. We illuminate each original with fixtures rated above CRI 99. The Color Rendering Index measures how accurately a light reveals true color, on a scale where 100 is perfect.

That neutral, full-spectrum light is what lets color read true. We capture a calibration test image at the beginning of each capture sequence, giving every image that follows a known baseline.

Color & restoration

Their true colors

Color doesn't just fade, it shifts. The dye layers in slides, negatives, and prints break down at different rates as they age, and because the cyan layer usually fails first, images drift toward magenta and red. We rebalance the individual color channels to recover a natural appearance as close as reasonably possible to the original. Spotting out dust and scratches, repairing tears, and rebuilding damaged areas is a separate restoration service we quote case by case.

FAQ

Questions we hear most

What can you scan?

Prints, slides, transparencies, and negatives, black-and-white or color, from 35mm up to large-format sheet film and oversized prints.

My slides or negatives are faded or color-shifted. Can you fix that?

Usually, yes, though it is a separate service from the scan itself. The scan records your slides and negatives as they are; bringing faded or color-shifted film back toward its original color is a dedicated correction pass we do afterward and price separately. Send us a sample and we'll let you know what is possible.

Will scanning damage my originals?

No. Everything is cleaned and handled by hand, with nothing pulled through an automatic feeder, so fragile, curled, or mounted originals are protected. Your originals come back in the same condition they arrived.

What resolution will my scans be?

Every image is captured at the full resolution of our cameras, using the entire sensor, with no cropping, downsizing, or compression. We hold every scan to that same maximum, so nothing is thrown away and your whole collection stays uniform, which makes it far easier to organize and work with later.

What will I receive?

Archival TIFF masters plus JPEG copies for easy sharing, organized to match your originals.

Can you handle a large collection?

Yes. From a single image to collections of many thousands, sorted and tracked so the files map back to your originals.

Do you remove dust and scratches?

Basic cleaning is part of scanning. Spotting, tear repair, and deeper restoration are available as separate services, quoted by condition.
